QUESTIONS FUTURE OWNERS ASK

Know what you're buying — and what you're not.

Brango is designed for people who want to build a client base and own a real B2B screening business, not for someone looking for a passive push-button income product.

Do I need background check industry experience?+

No. Sales, recruiting, HR, staffing or relationship-building experience can be extremely useful, while Brango provides the infrastructure and operating support behind the screening business.

Is this just software or a Chrome extension?+

No. The technology is part of the infrastructure. The core opportunity is owning and growing your own branded background check company, while Brango supports the software, screening workflow, vendor infrastructure and backend operations.

What am I responsible for?+

You are responsible for building your business: choosing a market, prospecting, creating relationships, selling, pricing and retaining clients. Brango is designed to take much of the operational and technical burden off your plate.

What can my company sell?+

Your company can offer criminal background checks, drug testing, employment and education verifications, motor vehicle records, identity-related screening, healthcare screening and other employment-screening services supported by the platform.

What if a client needs an ATS or HRIS integration?+

That is where Brango's technology layer comes in. Integration capabilities are available when they help close or support an employer account, but they are not the main message of the business opportunity.
