QUESTIONS

What future owners usually want to know.

The main idea is simple: you build and own the customer relationships; Brango Connect gives you the infrastructure required to operate the screening business.

Do I need background check experience?+

Industry or B2B sales experience can be a major advantage, but the Brango Connect model is built so qualified entrepreneurs do not have to create the screening technology, vendor network and backend operations from scratch.

Is Brango Connect just software or a Chrome extension?+

No. The Chrome extension and integration tools are only technology capabilities. The core Brango Connect opportunity is the infrastructure to operate and grow your own branded background check company.

Whose brand does the client see?+

Your company is designed to stay in front of the client through a white-label experience. Brango Connect powers the infrastructure behind the scenes.

What can I sell?+

The platform supports background screening services such as criminal searches, drug testing, employment and education verifications, motor vehicle records, healthcare-related screening and other hiring checks.

What if a client asks for an ATS or HRIS integration?+

That is where the Brango Connect technology layer becomes valuable. Integration and candidate-capture options are available when a client opportunity requires them. See the Technology & Integrations page for details.
