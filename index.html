QUESTIONS

What people usually want to know.

Whether you need screening for your company or want to build a background check business, the starting point is understanding the workflow.

Is Brango Connect only the Chrome extension?+

No. The extension is one candidate-capture tool inside a broader background screening platform. Brango also supports screening products, workflow automation, portals, integrations and affiliate infrastructure.

What types of background checks can you support?+

The platform is designed around criminal screening, drug testing, employment and education verifications, motor vehicle records, healthcare screening, identity data and related hiring workflows.

Can Brango connect with an ATS or HRIS?+

Yes. Brango is designed to support API and workflow integrations so candidate data, orders, statuses and results can move between screening and hiring systems.

What does the affiliate program include?+

The program is designed around a white-label portal, vendor and workflow infrastructure, support, launch planning, outreach materials and hands-on assistance as the business gets started.
