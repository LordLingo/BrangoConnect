Six things people ask first.

Do I need background check experience?

No. All but one of our affiliates started with none. What matters is that you can sell. You do not have to build the technology, answer every client call, or process the check yourself.

Won’t I be competing with the other affiliates?

There are hundreds of background check companies in the U.S., and every one of them is a competitor before another affiliate is. What the other affiliates add is volume: it sets the vendor pricing and turnaround you sell with. There are no territories, so you choose your own niche and market.

What does it cost?

Brango is built to keep the cost of entry low: a one-time license covers the platform, the back office, and launch support. The full investment and fee schedule are shared on your call. SBA financing is available for qualified buyers. See what’s included.

What if I already sell background checks for someone else?

A call starts a conversation. It is not a resignation. This is about clients you win for your own company, not a plan for moving an employer’s current accounts. If an agreement with an employer restricts that, the agreement controls. Go independent.

Is Brango reselling another company’s platform?

No. Brango built and owns its screening software, and our own developers customize it for affiliates and their clients. Technology and integrations.

How fast can I launch?

Your branded portal and vendors are set up in weeks 1–2. From weeks 3–12 you run demos, and our top salesperson joins your first 10.

We don’t license to everyone.

This takes 20 to 30 hours a week. If you want one more thing to own on the side, we’ll say no. If you’re built to grow, we’ll build the rest.
