Five things reps ask first.

Do I have to leave my job to book a call?

No. A call starts a conversation. It is not a resignation and it is not a commitment.

Can I take the accounts I sell today?

This is about the clients you win for your own company. It is not a plan for moving an employer’s current accounts. If an agreement with an employer restricts that, the agreement controls.

What does it cost?

Brango is built to keep the cost of entry low: a one-time license covers the platform, the back office, and launch support. The full investment and fee schedule are shared on your call. SBA financing is available for qualified buyers.

Won’t I be competing with the other affiliates?

There are hundreds of background check companies in the U.S., and every one of them is a competitor before another affiliate is. What the other affiliates add is volume: it sets the vendor pricing and turnaround you sell with. There are no territories, so you choose your own niche and market.

Who handles the checks and the client calls?

Brango does. Brango answers your clients’ calls and emails and processes the full background check. Every order runs through one portal under your brand.
