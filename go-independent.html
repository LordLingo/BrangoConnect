No. There is no upfront fee and no fixed startup price. Brango earns a fee on each search, plus a monthly revenue share of 7%, 12%, or 15%, depending on volume.

This is about the clients you win for your own company. It is not a plan for moving an employer's current accounts. If an agreement with an employer restricts that, the agreement controls.
