What brokers usually ask.

The buyer owns the company and the client relationships. Brango runs the back office. See what's included.

Is this a business for sale?+

No. It is a license to launch a new background screening company under the buyer's own brand. Brango builds the brand, website and toll-free number, and runs the back office.

What does it cost my buyer?+

Brango is built to keep the cost of entry low: a one-time license covers the platform, the back office, and launch support. The full investment and fee schedule are shared on the buyer's discovery call. SBA financing is available for qualified buyers.

Does my buyer need background check experience?+

No. Sales experience is the strong fit, in screening or in B2B. The buyer does not have to build the technology, answer every client call, or process the check.

Where can my buyer operate?+

Anywhere in the United States. There are no territories, protected zones, or zip-code limits.

How does partnering work for my brokerage?+

Referral terms are discussed on a call. Book a time or send the form and Brango replies by email.

Will Brango take every buyer I send?+

No. Brango is selective and does not license to everyone. Every buyer gets a call about fit. If it is not a fit, Brango says so.
